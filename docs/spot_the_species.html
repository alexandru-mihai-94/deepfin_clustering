You vs. DEEPFIN: name that clownfish

Nemo comes in many shapes and colors, A. Ocellaris and A. Percula are two sister clownfish species that can be difficult to identify. Given the two wild type examples can you name the species?

DEEPFIN was never told which fish is which. It looked at photos of 68 clownfish, one per fish, and sorted them into groups on its own. Below is its map: each photo sits where DEEPFIN placed it, and similar-looking fish end up close together. The coloured borders show the experts' labels, which we only added afterwards.

DEEPFIN can read the photos with different neural networks, and each draws its own map. Pick one:

■ Ocellaris   ■ Percula · outlined: the 10 quiz fish · hover or tap a photo to enlarge

The tell? Experts count the spines on the back fin and look at how thick the black outlines are. These two sister species split about 5 million years ago and can still interbreed, so even trained eyes hesitate.

DEEPFIN also sorts baby clownfish into their growth stages, with no labels needed, which could save scientists weeks of work on thousands of images. Read the paper in iScience · Code on GitHub
